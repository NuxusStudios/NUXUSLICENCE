Private by design

Your data stays yours

Polis Pass isn't built to collect you — it's built to protect you. The architecture minimizes data by default.

  • ✓You control sharing. Each time, you approve exactly what a verifier sees.
  • ✓The record stays with the ministry. Polis Pass shows it to you; it isn't a second database to breach.
  • ✓Verified offline. A tamper-proof, time-limited proof — no tracking, no central lookup.
  • ✓Stored in your country. Data residency per jurisdiction — and we never sell or monetize your data. Full stop.

Questions

Good to know

Is Polis Pass an official government app?

Polis Pass is an independent platform currently in prototype, designed for governments to adopt. Ontario is our first target market; it uses demo data until data-sharing agreements are in place, and it is not yet an official government service.

Is it free?

Yes — Polis Pass is free for residents. The platform is licensed to government, not paid for by you.

What happens if I lose my phone?

You can revoke your wallet instantly, which blocks any further use of your credentials from that device. Re-verify on a new device to restore access.

Does a digital licence replace my physical one?

Where accepted, it's a convenient companion. Keep your physical documents until an issuing authority confirms the digital version is accepted for a given use.

How is my privacy protected?

Polis Pass minimizes data by design: selective disclosure, offline verification, storage in Canada, and no sale of your data. See the Privacy section above.
