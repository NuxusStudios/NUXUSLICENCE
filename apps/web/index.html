Questions

Good to know

Is CivicPass an official Government of Ontario app?

CivicPass is an independent product currently in prototype. It is designed to work with Ontario's ministries and uses demo data until data-sharing agreements are in place. It is not yet an official government service.

Is it free?

Yes — CivicPass is free for residents. The platform is licensed to government, not paid for by you.

What happens if I lose my phone?

You can revoke your wallet instantly, which blocks any further use of your credentials from that device. Re-verify on a new device to restore access.

Does a digital licence replace my physical one?

Where accepted, it's a convenient companion. Keep your physical documents until an issuing authority confirms the digital version is accepted for a given use.

How is my privacy protected?

CivicPass minimizes data by design: selective disclosure, offline verification, storage in Canada, and no sale of your data. See the Privacy section above.
